# Privacy Policy

**Cimo - Media Optimizer** (the "Extension")

**Last updated:** July 10, 2026

## Summary

Cimo - Media Optimizer processes images entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data.

## What the Extension does

When you select or drop an image, the Extension:

1. Reads the image file from your device
2. Converts it to WebP format locally in your browser
3. Downloads the optimized file to your device

All processing happens inside the Extension popup. Your images are not uploaded to any server.

## Data collection

**We do not collect any data.**

The Extension does not:

- Upload images or files to external servers
- Send analytics or usage events
- Use cookies or tracking pixels
- Request access to websites you visit
- Read browsing history
- Access your filesystem beyond the file you choose

## Permissions

The Extension requests **no special Chrome permissions**. It does not use `host_permissions`, `content_scripts`, or background network access.

## Data storage

The Extension does not persist your images. Temporary data exists only in memory while the popup is open and is cleared when you close it.

Settings such as WebP quality and max dimension are stored locally in the Extension popup session only (not synced or transmitted).

## Third-party services

The Extension does not connect to third-party services during normal operation.

Source code dependencies are used only at build time and are not loaded from external servers when you use the Extension.

## Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this document in the project repository.
